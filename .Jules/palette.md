## 2026-08-01 - Fintech "Use Max" Quick Fill Pattern
**Learning:** For fintech investment platforms, users appreciate reducing friction when withdrawing their exact balance. Providing a "Use Max" quick-fill button integrated directly with the available balance status inline text improves usability drastically and prevents manually typing long numbers. It must be dynamically managed based on the pending status notice state.
**Action:** Always provide instant fill/helper buttons near numerical inputs for balances, with proper ARIA labels and dynamic disabled status based on global form state.
