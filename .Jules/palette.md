# Palette's Journal - EASYPIE UX/Accessibility Insights

Welcome to the UX/Accessibility design journal for EASYPIE.

## 2025-02-15 - Unified Copy Success Feedback and Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Standardizing feedback across high-frequency actions prevents disruptive modal-based alerts (like `alert()`) and elevates the perceived luxury of a platform. Additionally, critical mobile navigation and action headers containing icon-only buttons require explicit `aria-label` definitions to ensure a consistent experience for keyboard and screen-reader users.
**Action:** Replace `alert()` messages with elegant, styled, inline or floating toast feedback and ensure all icon-only headers use `aria-label` properties.
