## 2025-02-15 - Label-Input Pairing for Mobile Fintech Forms
**Learning:** In mobile-first, dark-themed applications like EASYPIE, form-field touch targets are crucial. When `<label>` elements lack explicit `for` attributes pairing them with their input/select elements, mobile users lose the ability to focus inputs by tapping on the labels, reducing the accessible interactive area. Additionally, screen readers are left unable to form secure accessibility associations, which violates standard accessibility guidelines.
**Action:** Always ensure that every `<label>` has a matching `for` attribute pointing to the unique `id` of its corresponding input, select, or textarea control. This maximizes the touch/click target size and guarantees seamless screen-reader compatibility.
